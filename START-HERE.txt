AFTER THE MISSION
A Moon and Mars museum for NASA Space Apps 2026

OPEN YOUR WEBSITE
1. If you downloaded the ZIP, right-click it and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted after-the-mission folder.
3. Open website, then double-click index.html.
4. Your browser opens the museum. Keep all files together.
No installation, account, API key, or terminal is needed.
NASA links and optional Google fonts require internet access.

WHAT TO TRY
Enter the museum. Search for a mission or switch between Moon and Mars.
Open an exhibit and inspect its original image. Download a field note.
Explore the timeline and complete the three-question field challenge.
Visited exhibits are remembered when your browser permits local storage.
Field notes save in your browser when local storage is available. Download a copy to share or preserve them.
